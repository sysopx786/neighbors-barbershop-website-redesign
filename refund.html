Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Appointments

Appointments are booked through Square Appointments. Please cancel or reschedule as early as possible so we can offer the time to someone else.

  • Cancellations more than 24 hours in advance: no charge.
  • Late cancellations (under 24 hours) or no-shows: we may charge a fee or require payment for the reserved time at the shop’s discretion.
  • Walk-ins are first-come, first-served when capacity allows.

Service Satisfaction

If you are not satisfied with your cut, speak to us before you leave the shop. We will do our best to make it right (adjustments at no extra charge when reasonable). Once you leave, we cannot guarantee free re-dos.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Square. Refunds, if approved, are issued through Square according to their process. Contact the shop first at (267) 566-7871 or theneighborsbarber@gmail.com.

Gift Cards / Packages

If offered in the future, specific terms will be stated at purchase. Currently none.

Questions? Call or email us. See also our Terms and Privacy Policy.
