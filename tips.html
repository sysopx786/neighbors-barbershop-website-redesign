Tips

Between visits

What we say in the chair.

Four notes for the days you’re not in it. Not a product list. Not a routine you have to keep. Just how a fade, a beard, a razor shave, and a kid’s cut actually behave after you stand up.

  1. A skin fade grows out. Let it.
  2. The neckline is the whole beard.
  3. After a head shave.
  4. Before a kid sits down.

Weeks 2 and 3

A skin fade grows out. Let it.

A skin fade is tightest the day you leave the chair. Week one it still reads as a fade. By week two the hairline fills back in and the blend looks closer to a low taper. That is still the haircut. It is not a mistake.

Leave the sides alone. A home clipper pass along the temple is how a clean fade turns into a stripe. If the top needs help, a little product is enough to hold the shape you left with. Don’t recut the blend.

Most regulars come back around week three. Some wait four if they keep more length on top. If you want it looking like the day you sat down, book closer to two weeks. If you’re fine with it grown in and still clean, three is honest.

  • Men’s Skin Fade · $40
  • Skin Fade & Beard · $45

Between cuts

The neckline is the whole beard.

What makes a beard look unfinished is almost never the length. It’s the neck.

The line sits just above where the neck starts to curve under the jaw, not halfway down the throat. Don’t chase it lower every week. You’ll end up with a patch and nowhere to put the line back. Leave the cheek line where we set it.

At home, only take bulk from the middle, and stop before you think you’re done. After a shower, pat it dry. If the skin feels tight, a few drops of oil, worked from the skin out. That’s the routine. Not a shelf of products.

Between haircuts, a beard-only visit is twenty minutes. Hot towel, lines reset. You don’t need a haircut to book it.

  • Beard Only · $20
  • Traditional & Beard · $40

The first night

After a head shave.

The scalp is skin that hasn’t seen a razor much. The first night, skip a tight hat and skip anything with alcohol in it. If it feels tight, a little unscented moisturizer. That’s the aftercare.

Don’t shave it again the next morning to keep it smooth. Give the skin a day. Shadow in two or three days is normal. If it itches, it’s usually dry, not hair growing the wrong way. Moisturizer, not scratching.

Clippers at home will leave a shadow the razor didn’t. If you want it this clean again, come back for the shave.

If the skin is broken, red past the usual, or reacting, leave it alone and tell us before the next razor.

  • Head Shave · $30
  • Head & Beard Shave · $50

On the way over

Before a kid sits down.

Decide the cut with them on the way over, if they’re old enough to care. A photo on a phone is more useful than “shorter on the sides.”

They can say stop. We go at their pace. Parents can stay by the chair. First cut or fiftieth, same idea. Kids already know they can walk in for a water even when they’re not getting cut.

If they’re nervous, a slower part of the day is easier than a packed Saturday. Call and ask what’s open.

Twelve and under. Any style, including a simple buzz. The bench is there if a sibling is waiting.

  • Kid’s Haircut (12 & Under) · $30

Hair grows differently on every head. If something here doesn’t match what you see in the mirror, ask when you sit down. Prices are the same as the board. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is open.
