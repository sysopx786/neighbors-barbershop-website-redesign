What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They help sites remember preferences or understand usage.

Cookies We Use

This site currently does not set non-essential cookies of its own. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookie deployed by Neighbors Barbershop.

  • Essential / functional: None set by us for site operation beyond standard hosting.
  • Third-party: Google Fonts (if loaded) and the embedded Google Map may set cookies under Google’s policies. The Book page embeds Square’s booking page, which may set cookies under Square’s policies.

This site also stores two small settings in your browser’s localStorage: your light or dark theme choice and whether you dismissed the cookie notice. Neither is a cookie, and neither is sent to us or to anyone else.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect map embedding or font loading but the core site will still work. See your browser’s help for instructions.

Consent

If we add analytics or other tools later, we will update this policy and add a proper consent mechanism.
