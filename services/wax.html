Ear, Nose Wax & Eyebrow Sculpting

$5 · 10 min

The small stuff that makes the whole look cleaner.

Ears, nose, brows. One, two, or all three, about 10 minutes, $5. We use a skin-safe wax and keep the brows natural, not thin or overdone. We’ll check with you before we take more.

It’s quick. Most guys say it’s worth the few seconds. A little redness can happen and usually fades fast. Sensitive skin or allergies? Tell us first.

Add it to any cut or shave, or come in for it by itself. Most guys do it every 3 to 6 weeks. It’s already included in The Neighborhood Standard.
