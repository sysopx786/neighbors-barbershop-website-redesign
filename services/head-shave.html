Services

Head Shave

$30 · 30 min

Smoother than any buzz. That’s the point.

We start with hot lather and a hot towel with essential oils to soften everything. Then a straight razor across the whole scalp, slow and careful. A second hot towel, then a moisturizing scrub so you don’t walk out feeling dry or tight.

Got sensitive skin, a mole, a scar, or bumps? Point it out before we start and we’ll work around it. Still have hair up there? No problem. We shave whatever’s there.

It’s about 30 minutes. The smooth usually lasts a day or two, and a lot of guys book it every week or two. Want the beard done too? Book Head & Beard Shave.
