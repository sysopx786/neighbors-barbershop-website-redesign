Men’s Skin Fade

$40 · 30 min

A skin fade is all detail work. That’s the part we care about.

Tell us where you want it to start, low, mid, or high, and how much you want left on top. We take it down to the skin with a smooth blend, clean up the edges, and hand you the mirror so you can check the back before you go. Not right? Say so while you’re still in the chair and we’ll fix it.

It’s about 30 minutes, and nobody’s rushing you out. Talk about your week, your kid’s game, whatever. Traditional barbering in a modern era. That’s the whole idea.

Most guys come back every 2 to 3 weeks to keep it sharp. And the bench out front is yours either way.
