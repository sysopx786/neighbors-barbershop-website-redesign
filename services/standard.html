Services

The Neighborhood Standard

$75 · 1 hr

The full version of what we do.

Any haircut on the board: skin fade, traditional, whatever you want. Then a beard trim with a hot towel, full waxing on the ears, nose, and brows, a light facial moisturizer, and a cold press to finish. Not much beard? We still clean up what’s there.

It’s an hour, and we use all of it. You leave looking finished: hair, beard, and skin.

Some guys book it monthly. Some save it for a wedding, an event, or a reset after too long between cuts. One visit, one price. Tell us the cut and the beard shape when you sit down, and we’ll take care of the rest.
